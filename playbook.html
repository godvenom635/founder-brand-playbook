THE FOUNDER POSITIONING STATEMENT

Founder A: I help bootstrapped SaaS founders go from $0 → $10K MRR using founder-led content — and I believe paid ads kill more startups than they save.
Founder B: I help D2C brands go from 6 → 7 figures using founder origin videos — and I believe agencies are the worst place to learn marketing.
Founder C: I help service businesses replace cold outreach with inbound DMs — and I believe sales teams are a $1M+ revenue problem, not a $0–$1M one.
1
The 1-line stranger test

Can a stranger explain what you do in one sentence after watching one of your videos? If not, you're not positioned — you're posting.

2
The DM mirror test

Do followers DM you with the exact problem you said you solve? If your DMs are random, your positioning is random.

3
The "no" test

Are you turning down clients who don't fit? Founders with sharp positioning say no more than yes. That's the moat.

4
The disagreement test

Could 3 people in your niche publicly disagree with your belief? If not, you don't have a belief — you have a description.

IDEATION
never run out of ideas
THE 5 BUCKETS.
Every founder content idea comes from one of these five places.
1
CONTRARIAN

Things you believe nobody else in your space is saying. Most founders are too scared to publish these — that's exactly why they work. One contrarian post = 100 safe posts.

2
LESSONS

Things you wish someone told you 12 months ago. Frame them as: "If I started over today, here's what I'd do differently." Easy to write, instantly relatable, perfect for hooks.

3
PROCESS

Things you do every day that look obvious to you but are magic to your audience. The "how I do X in 10 mins" video. Founders underestimate how valuable their boring routines are.

4
OBJECTIONS

Things prospects say on sales calls — every "why I'm hesitant" objection is a video. Your sales calls are the cheapest market research you'll ever do. Record them. Mine them.

5
COMMENTARY

Things in the news in your industry. React, disagree, predict. You don't need to be the source — being the smart filter is enough to be followed.

★
THE 1 → 20 RULE

One real conversation = 20 pieces of content. If you can't pull 20 ideas out of a single sales call, podcast, or DM thread — you're not paying attention.

WHERE TO CAPTURE IDEAS
DAILY
Voice memo after every sales call. 60 seconds. What did they push back on? What did they get excited about? That's tomorrow's video.
DAILY
One Notes file called "IDEAS". Add to it as you scroll. Don't categorize. Don't perfect. Just dump.
WEEKLY
10-min "what surprised me" prompt. Open a doc, set a 10-min timer, write what surprised you this week. 70% of those become posts.
WEEKLY
Mine your DMs. Every question = a video. If 3 people asked it, 3,000 are wondering it.

SCRIPTING
how to write a video that holds
HOOK → TENSION
→ PAYOFF.
Every viral founder script. Three parts. That's it.
THE FOUNDER SCRIPT FRAMEWORK
HOOK (0–3s) a claim, a contrarian line, or a sharp pain — must land in <1 second on screen.
TENSION (3–8s) "here's why most founders get this wrong" — name the enemy.
PAYOFF (8–40s) the actual answer. Specific. One idea. Not three.
CTA (last 5s) save / share / DM keyword. Always one ask, never two.
3 HOOK FORMULAS THAT NEVER MISS
A
"I USED TO…"

"I used to think hiring SDRs was the answer. Then I tried this for 90 days…" Confession + curiosity = unstoppable open.

B
"STOP DOING X"

"Stop hiring social media managers. Hire one editor and become the brand yourself." Direct. Authoritative. Polarizing.

C
"SPECIFIC NUMBER"

"We hit $400K in 6 months from one tactic everyone in our space ignores." Specifics signal proof. Numbers stop the scroll.

RULES I FOLLOW EVERY TIME
One idea per video. Never two. The second one becomes its own video.
No intros. Don't say "hey guys." Start with the hook on frame 1.
Read it out loud 3 times before you film. If it sounds written, rewrite it. Founders sound like founders, not blog posts.
30–90 seconds is the sweet spot for founder content. Long enough to teach. Short enough to finish.

FILMING + EDITING
production is leverage, not vanity
YOUR PHONE
IS ENOUGH.
The founders winning on IG don't out-produce. They out-ship.

FILMING — THE SETUP

  • Phone (iPhone 13 or up). Yes, just your phone. Stop buying gear.
  • Natural light facing a window. Mid-morning is the cheat code.
  • Tripod at chest height — eye-level, never up your nose.
  • Lavalier mic ($30 wired or $80 wireless). Audio is non-negotiable. People forgive bad video, never bad audio.
  • Plain background. No clutter. No moving shadows.
  • Film 5–10 takes per video. Pick the one where you sound most like yourself, not the most "polished" one.

EDITING — THE 80/20

  • Tools: CapCut (free, mobile) or Descript (desktop, AI-cuts). Skip Premiere until you're at 50K+ followers.
  • Cut every silence > 0.5 seconds. Pace = retention.
  • Subtitles always — 35% of viewers watch on mute.
  • B-roll every 3–5 seconds for retention. Phone footage of your screen, your office, your team.
  • One zoom-in on the most important sentence. Just one.
  • Hook lands in <1 sec after trim. Trim ruthlessly from the front.
The 30-min rule: if a 60-second video takes more than 30 minutes to edit, your editor (or you) is overcooking it. Cut, caption, ship. Founder content wins on volume + speed, not finish.
YOUR WEEKLY FILM DAY
SUN
Block 90 minutes. Same time every week. Treat it like an investor meeting — not a "when I have time" task.
SUN
Film 5 videos in one go. Same shirt. Same setup. Different angles + cuts make them feel different. Don't film alone all week — batch.
MON
Hand off raw footage to your editor by 10am. They edit M–W. You schedule Thursday. You ship Friday onwards.

THE 4 RULES NOBODY FOLLOWS
1
Don't post and ghost.

Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes. The algorithm scores early engagement velocity 10x more than late.

2
Re-share old wins.

A reel that hit 100K six months ago will hit again with a fresh hook. Founders waste their best assets by posting once.

3
DM 5 new followers daily.

Personal note. Not a pitch. "Saw you followed — what brought you here?" Builds the warmest list you'll ever own.

4
Comment on 5 bigger accounts daily.

Meaningful comments — not "great post 🔥". The comment section is the cheapest discovery loop on the internet right now.

Posting time matters less than you think. Post when you can reply. A 7am post you respond to beats a "perfect 9pm" post you ignore.

CLONE YOURSELF
you don't need an agency
CLONE YOURSELF.
A system that runs on you, without becoming you.
THE 4 ROLES YOU CLONE — IN THIS ORDER
1
THE IDEATOR

This is you. Forever. 30 minutes a week. Pull from the 5 buckets. Send voice memos. Never outsource opinion — only production.

2
THE WRITER

Turns your voice memos into scripts using your hook formulas. A junior writer (or a tight AI prompt with your past 50 posts as voice training) does this in under an hour per video.

3
THE EDITOR

Turns raw 5-take footage into ready-to-post videos. The single highest-leverage hire of your founder brand. Pay well below an agency, get 10x the speed.

4
THE COMMUNITY MANAGER

Schedules posts, replies in first 60 mins (in your voice), routes hot DMs to you, qualifies leads. Last hire. Don't hire this until you have 10K followers and real DM volume.

WHEN TO HIRE EACH
0 → 5K FOLLOWERSDo all 4 roles yourself. You're not too small to do it — you're too small to outsource it. Learn the work first, then delegate.
5K → 20KHire your first editor. Keep ideation, scripting, replying. The editor alone gives you 10 hours a week back.
20K+Add the writer + community manager. Build a tight 3-person team. You stay the IDEATOR. Forever.
The non-negotiable rule: your team can produce. Your team cannot have an opinion. The moment your voice stops sounding like you, your founder brand stops being a moat — it becomes a content factory, and content factories die in 90 days.
